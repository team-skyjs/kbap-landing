A menu translation that says **pancake** can leave you with several different choices. For these Korean savory pancakes, the name gives a useful clue: green onions, kimchi or a mung bean base. It does not give you the complete recipe.

## Pajeon: green onions, sometimes with seafood

**파전 (pajeon)** is a green onion pancake cooked in a pan. The Korea Tourism Organization's [pajeon description](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=179010) includes scallions, squid and clams with flour batter. A menu that simply says pajeon therefore does not establish that it contains only vegetables.

**해물파전 (haemul pajeon)** makes the seafood part explicit. KTO's [seafood and green onion pancake guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=231718) gives squid, shrimp and mussels as common examples. The particular seafood can differ, so check the restaurant's version if you have a preference or need to avoid an ingredient.

## Kimchijeon: kimchi in the batter

**김치전 (kimchijeon)** is a kimchi pancake. KTO's [kimchi pancake guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=178931) describes chopped kimchi mixed into a batter and cooked in an oiled pan. It also notes that pork or squid can be added.

The name tells you to expect kimchi, but it does not rule out meat or seafood. Ask about the other ingredients rather than treating the name as a complete description.

## Bindaetteok: a mung bean base

**빈대떡 (bindaetteok)** is a mung bean pancake. Its base is the useful distinction here: KTO's [mung bean pancake guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=178984) describes finely ground mung beans made into a batter and pan-fried. The version it describes also contains pork, mung bean sprouts and bracken.

A bean-based pancake is not necessarily a meat-free dish. The menu name points to the base, while the restaurant can explain what else goes into it.

## Use the name to ask a more specific question

| Menu name | Main clue | A useful follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| 파전 / 해물파전 | Green onions; 해물 explicitly indicates seafood | Which seafood is included? |
| 김치전 | Kimchi | Is pork or seafood added? |
| 빈대떡 | Ground mung beans | What else is mixed into the batter? |

If ingredients matter to your choice, ask about the batter, additions and dipping sauce separately. A dish name or photo cannot confirm the full recipe at that restaurant.
