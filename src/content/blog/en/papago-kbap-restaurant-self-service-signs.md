You have chosen your lunch, but a Korean notice beside the water station leaves you wondering what to do next. Papago's image translation can help you read the instruction. K-Bap is useful for the menu and the dishes you are choosing.

Self-service is part of some Korean restaurant visits. The Korea Tourism Organization's [restaurant guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=229195) describes places where customers collect their own water or side dishes. Follow the instructions at the restaurant you are visiting; arrangements differ.

## Photograph the notice in Papago

NAVER's [mobile image-translation guide](https://help.naver.com/service/16448/contents/5973?lang=en&osType=MOBILE) explicitly includes signboards. In the Papago mobile app:

1. Choose the language pair, such as Korean to English, on the home screen.
2. Open **Image translation** and photograph the notice.
3. Highlight the complete phrase you want to read with your finger, then check the translation.

If you already have the photo, open Image translation and use the **Gallery** icon to select it. These instructions concern the mobile app. Keep a network connection available: NAVER's [offline translation guide](https://help.naver.com/service/16448/contents/25501?lang=en&osType=COMMONOS) says image translation is not supported offline.

## Read what the instruction applies to

Imagine a notice describing where customers should collect water. Check that the translated instruction refers to water, and look for any direction or condition on the same sign. A word suggesting self-service does not by itself explain every service arrangement in the restaurant.

Include the whole phrase when selecting text. NAVER's [image-translation troubleshooting guide](https://help.naver.com/service/16448/contents/5981?lang=en&osType=MOBILE) says incomplete selections, handwriting and background patterns can interfere with recognition. If the result leaves the action unclear, show the sign to staff and ask for help.

For a spoken reply that you need to understand, see our guide to [restaurant follow-up questions with Papago](/blog/papago-kbap-restaurant-follow-up-questions/).

## Use K-Bap for the food choice

When your question is about a dish on the menu, K-Bap can scan a menu photo and show information and pictures for recognized dishes, as described in its [official app listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/k-bap/id6788635067).

Use the restaurant notice to understand the action expected of you, and the dish information to decide what you want to eat. Our [Photo and List guide](/blog/switch-photo-list-menu-scan-kbap/) explains how to revisit the menu image while checking a K-Bap result. [Explore K-Bap](/) before your next restaurant visit.
