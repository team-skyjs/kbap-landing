You have scanned a Korean menu, but want another look at the small print beside one dish. You can return to the photo from the result screen without taking another picture. K-Bap's **Photo** and **List** tabs let you move between the menu image and the dish results from that scan.

## Open Photo from the result screen

After a successful menu scan, K-Bap opens the **List** view. At the top of the results, tap **Photo** to see the image used for the scan.

The Photo view shows the menu picture itself. Use it to locate the Korean line you were reading, including nearby size labels, options or prices that help distinguish it from another item.

To look closer:

- Spread two fingers on the image to zoom in.
- Drag the enlarged image to move across the menu.
- Double-tap to switch between an enlarged view and the whole-photo view.

Zooming makes the existing image larger. If the letters in the photo are blurred or obscured by glare, check the printed menu directly or take a clearer photo.

## Return to List to compare the dish

Tap **List** to go back to the dish results. Compare the item you are considering with the line you just checked in Photo. For example, two similar-looking rows on a menu may differ by a size or an extra ingredient mentioned beside the name.

The food pictures in List serve a different purpose from the menu image in Photo. K-Bap's [official app listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/k-bap/id6788635067) describes showing food photos for recognized dishes. Those pictures help explain the kind of food; they do not establish the exact serving at the restaurant in front of you.

If a dish could not be identified, see [what to do with an unmatched menu item](/blog/kbap-menu-item-not-identified/). For a price you want to double-check, our [guide to won prices and K-Bap's display currency](/blog/read-menu-prices-korean-won-kbap/) explains which amount to compare with the menu.

## Choose after checking

Continue choosing dishes from List once the menu line makes sense to you. If an option is still unclear, point to the line and ask staff before adding it to your order. [Explore K-Bap](/) to see how menu photos, dish information and a Korean order screen fit together.
