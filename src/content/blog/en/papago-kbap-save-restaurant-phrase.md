At a restaurant in Korea, you might ask, “Can I get this to go?” Two days later, at a different counter, you need the same Korean sentence. Papago Favorites lets you keep the translation for that moment. If what you want to remember is the stew you ordered, save the dish in K-Bap instead.

## Keep a question you can use at another counter

In the Papago mobile app, choose English as the source language and Korean as the target. Type your question and translate it. When you have the wording you want, save the translation to **Favorites**. NAVER explains both steps in its guides to [translating typed text](https://help.naver.com/service/16448/contents/17747?lang=en&osType=MOBILE) and [saving translations in Favorites](https://help.naver.com/service/16448/contents/5977?lang=en&osType=MOBILE).

For example, **이거 포장할 수 있나요?** asks “Can I get this to go?” You can point to a dish on the menu as you show the question. A phrase like this is easy to reuse because the dish, quantity and price can change without changing what you ask. If you save a sentence that specifies two portions, check the number before showing it again.

## Find the sentence when you need it

Open **Favorites** in Papago and look for the question you saved. You can match the English wording to the Korean translation before turning the screen toward the person at the counter. NAVER says previously saved translations are available there even without a network connection. Save the question while you have a connection if you expect to need it later.

The question leaves room for the restaurant to answer. If staff offer a different packaging option or ask which dish you mean, our guide to [restaurant follow-up questions](/blog/papago-kbap-restaurant-follow-up-questions/) covers that next exchange.

## Remember the dish for another meal

Suppose you liked **된장찌개 (doenjang jjigae)** and want to recognize its name on another menu. In K-Bap, sign in, open the dish detail and tap the star. Later, open **Profile → Saved** to find that dish again. See the [guide to saving Korean dishes](/blog/save-korean-dishes-kbap/) for the full steps.

On your next visit, open Papago for the takeaway question. Open the [K-Bap](/) saved dish when it is the Korean food name you need to show or recognize.
