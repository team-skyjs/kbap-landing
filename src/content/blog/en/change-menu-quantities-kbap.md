You meant to select one bowl, but the number beside it says two. Or someone at the table has changed their mind. In K-Bap, change the quantity in the scanned menu's **List** view before showing your order to staff.

If you have already opened **Your order**, use its back arrow to return to the scan results. The order card displays your selection; the list is where you can adjust it.

## Use the controls beside the right menu item

Find the dish in **List**, checking its name against the restaurant's menu. If you need to see the original image again, our [Photo and List guide](/blog/switch-photo-list-menu-scan-kbap/) explains that switch.

- Tap **+** beside an unselected item to add one.
- Once selected, the row shows **−**, the quantity, and **+**. Tap **+** to increase the quantity by one or **−** to reduce it by one.
- To remove an item from your selection, reduce its quantity from one to zero. The row returns to its single **+** control. The dish stays in the scan results, so you can add it again if needed.

For example, if a row shows three and you want two, tap **−** once and check that it now shows two. This is an example of the quantity control, not a recommendation about how much food to order.

When the list shows separate size or quantity options, use the control beside the option you actually want. Increasing a number is not a substitute for checking which menu option you selected.

## Reopen the card after making a change

With at least one item selected, tap **View order** at the bottom of the scan results. Check the dishes and quantities on **Your order** again. Returning from the card, editing the list and reopening the card lets you review the updated selection before handing over your phone.

If you remove every selected item, the View order control disappears until you add something again. That does not mean the menu scan itself has been deleted.

For the next step, see [how to show your food order in Korean](/blog/show-food-order-in-korean/). The app's [official listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/k-bap/id6788635067) describes the Korean order screen as something you show to restaurant staff.

## A change in the app is not a change at the restaurant

If staff have already taken your order, tell them directly what you want to change and ask whether it is still possible. Editing the app's selection does not notify the kitchen or cancel food that is being prepared.

Check the restaurant's portion rules, availability and final price with staff. K-Bap's displayed total is an estimate; changing the quantity does not confirm a new bill. Our [guide to menu prices and display currency](/blog/read-menu-prices-korean-won-kbap/) explains that distinction.
